The expert

Twenty years in practice.
Instructed both sides.

Dr David Silver is a Chartered Physiotherapist with a PhD in concussion research and over 20 years of active clinical practice, with specialist expertise across musculoskeletal injury, concussion and sports medicine, including a particular focus on youth populations and the independent school sector. He provides independent medico-legal reports and attends court as an expert witness in proceedings across England and Wales.

His opinions are formed from active clinical practice rather than historic knowledge, and examinations are conducted at The Forge Clinic in Richmond upon Thames and Athena Physiotherapy in Cobham, Surrey. Reports are clearly structured, evidence-based and delivered to the timescales agreed at the point of instruction.

Instructions are accepted from claimant and defendant solicitors on identical terms. All reports include a full CPR Part 35 declaration, and court availability is confirmed before instructions are accepted. Dr Silver is a registered member of the Expert Witness Institute.

Areas of expertise

Accepted instructions
in three disciplines.

All instructions fall within David's active clinical practice — meaning that opinions reflect current, hands-on expertise rather than knowledge that has not been tested against clinical reality in recent years.

Musculoskeletal Injury

Assessment and opinion on soft tissue, joint and spinal injuries arising from accidents, workplace incidents, and sports.

  • Spinal injuries — cervical, thoracic, lumbar
  • Soft tissue — ligament, tendon, muscle
  • Joint injuries — shoulder, knee, hip, ankle
  • Chronic pain and functional overlay
  • Causation and prognosis
  • Road traffic accidents
  • Employer liability claims

Concussion & Brain Health

Doctoral-level expertise in concussion assessment, management and long-term consequences — an increasingly significant area in personal injury litigation.

  • Mild traumatic brain injury (mTBI)
  • Post-concussion syndrome
  • Sport-related concussion
  • Return-to-play and return-to-work assessment
  • Cognitive sequelae
  • Causation disputes in head injury cases
  • Youth and adult populations — including school-age claimants

Sports Medicine

Specialist opinion on injuries sustained in sport, exercise and physical training — including professional athlete claims and sports liability cases.

  • Sports injury causation and mechanism
  • Overuse and cumulative injury
  • Professional athlete disability assessment
  • Duty of care in sports organisations
  • Coaching and supervision liability
  • Equipment and facility liability
  • Return to sport capacity
  • Youth sport injury and safeguarding duty cases
  • Independent school sector — duty of care and supervision liability

Approach & independence

Objective opinion.
Clearly expressed.
Defensible in court.

Reports express David's genuine clinical opinion, formed on the evidence presented and not shaped by the identity or instructions of the retaining party. Instructions are accepted from claimant and defendant solicitors on entirely identical terms.

Examinations are conducted thoroughly, with records reviewed in full before the claimant is seen. Where opinion is provisional, or where it would be assisted by further information or investigation, this is stated explicitly within the report. Reports are structured so that the clinical reasoning can be followed by a judge or barrister without specialist knowledge.

01

Independent of both parties

Instructions are accepted from claimant and defendant solicitors on entirely identical terms, and opinion is not influenced by the identity or position of the instructing party.

02

Clearly structured, well-reasoned

Reports are structured to meet CPR Part 35 requirements, with clinical reasoning set out explicitly, the basis for each opinion clearly stated, and the limits of expertise acknowledged where relevant.

03

Available for court attendance

Court availability is confirmed before instructions are accepted, and diary dates are protected accordingly. Attendance at trial, pre-trial reviews and joint expert discussions is provided as required by the case.

04

Agreed turnaround, met

Report deadlines are agreed at the point of instruction and adhered to. Where circumstances arise that may affect delivery, this is communicated at the earliest opportunity rather than at the deadline itself.

Instruction process

From instruction
to delivery.

The instruction process is straightforward and responses are prompt from the point of initial contact.

01

Initial Enquiry

Initial enquiry by email with a brief case outline and the scope of instruction required. Availability and fee confirmation is provided within 48 hours of the initial contact.

02

Letter of Instruction

Formal letter of instruction received with the relevant records, witness statements and any joint expert directions. All records are reviewed in full before the examination is conducted.

03

Examination

Examination conducted at The Forge Clinic in Richmond upon Thames or Athena Physiotherapy in Cobham, Surrey. Remote examination is available where directed by the court or agreed between the parties.

04

Report & Court

Draft report issued to the instructing solicitor for factual accuracy check, with the final report produced to the agreed deadline. Court dates are diary-protected on acceptance of instructions, and joint statements are prepared as directed.

CPR Part 35 declaration

Part 35 declaration.

I understand that my overriding duty is to the court, and that this duty overrides any obligation I may have to those instructing me or to the party by whom I am paid.

I have complied and will comply with this duty. I am aware of the requirements of CPR Part 35, the Practice Direction and the Protocol for the Instruction of Experts to give Evidence in Civil Claims.

The opinions I have expressed represent my true and complete professional opinion on the matters to which they refer. I have indicated the facts and assumptions upon which those opinions are based, and I have not omitted to consider material facts which could detract from my concluded opinion.

Standard declaration included in all expert reports — Dr David Silver PhD, BSc, MSc, Chartered Physiotherapist
