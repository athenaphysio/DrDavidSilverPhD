What makes this different

Not a speaker who read
some articles. A clinician
who did the research.

Most corporate wellbeing is delivered by people who are one of three things: a clinician who is academically credible but dry in a room, a motivational speaker who is engaging but light on evidence, or a coach with a personal story but no scientific grounding. Very few are all three — and fewer still have conducted their own original research to underpin everything they deliver.

David brings PhD-level research in concussion and brain health, 20 years of active clinical practice, and a genuine ability to make an audience feel something shift — not just leave better informed. The clinical science is always in the room, but it lands because it arrives alongside genuine human warmth and connection.

He has worked with organisations across technology, media, finance, sport and the public sector, adapting the same evidence base to speak directly to the specific pressures and culture of each audience.

The research behind the work

Built on original insight,
not assembled content.

David’s corporate wellbeing work is informed by an ongoing qualitative research project with senior HR professionals — conducted because personal conversations with his wife and sister, both senior HR leaders, showed him that the people most responsible for organisational wellbeing are often the least supported themselves.

The findings from that research shape every corporate session David delivers, and will be published as a white paper in 2027. No other speaker in this market is building from this foundation — and it changes the quality of the conversation in the room.

How engagements work

Every engagement starts
with a conversation.

David does not deliver generic wellbeing content. Before any engagement is confirmed, he wants to understand the audience — their pressures, their culture, what has been tried before and what did not work, and what a genuinely successful session would look like for the organisation.

That conversation shapes everything: the talk selected, the examples used, the language adopted, and the pace and format of the session itself. The clinical science is constant. Everything else is adapted.

Post-session follow-up is available on request, and David is open to ongoing consultancy relationships with organisations who want to move beyond one-off sessions towards a more sustained shift in culture and performance.
