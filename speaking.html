Why this is different

Information doesn't change behaviour.
Emotion does.

Most workplace wellbeing sessions are well-intentioned and completely forgettable. People nod along, collect a handout, and return to their desks unchanged. That isn't a content problem. It's a connection problem.

I've spent 20 years in clinical practice sitting with people at their most vulnerable — in pain, exhausted, overwhelmed, or recovering from serious injury. I understand the human body deeply. But more than that, I understand people. What it takes to make someone feel genuinely seen. What has to happen in a room before a person is ready to change something.

The clinical science is always present. The PhD is in the room. But audiences leave not just knowing more — they feel something shift. That's what creates lasting change. And that's what moves your wellbeing metrics.

The talk menu

Four sessions.
All built from clinical reality.

Each talk is designed to stand alone or work as part of a programme. All can be delivered as a 30-minute keynote, a 60-minute lunch & learn, or a 90-minute interactive workshop. Click each to expand.

Talk 01
Perform Under Pressure
The deep secrets modern medicine doesn't want you to know.
Keynote Workshop 30–90 min

Burnout isn't a character flaw. It's a physiological process — one that follows predictable patterns and, crucially, one that can be understood and interrupted. This session translates the neuroscience of stress and recovery into language that high-performing professionals can actually use.

We look at what happens in the body under sustained pressure, why the standard advice ("take a break", "practice mindfulness") often fails in high-demand environments, and what the evidence actually suggests about recovery, regulation, and resilience.

Audience leaves with
  • A clear understanding of pain neuroscience and how modern medicine has set a poor precedent.
  • Explore the vast influences on our subconscious safety and danger decision making
  • A different relationship with the concept of "pushing through"
Talk 02
The Physical Workplace
Why movement and posture are performance tools, not HR box-ticking
Keynote Workshop 30–90 min

Honed at the coal face of point of care medicine, David shares his most challenging, emotional and rewarding experiences. He explores the skills and techniques gained over two decades that have made him one of the most impactful and sought after therapists.

Through the most practical examples, David defines how evidenced-based but simple techniques can help leaders shape relationships, and foster positive change.

Audience leaves with
  • Understand how subjective views shape our physical and emotional relationships
  • Explore how subtle language choices can frame behaviours
  • Develop practical structures that foster personal empowerment in us and others
Talk 03
Head in the Game
Brain health, cognitive performance and what every professional needs to understand
Keynote Workshop 30–90 min

Drawing directly on the latest research, this session explores how and why our nervous system has been consistently overlooked by medics and its remarkable power when understood and harnessed.

This is not a mental health awareness session. David dives deep into the system that shapes our world through sensation, movement and cognition. Real world examples help recover our lost relationship with our most vital asset.

Audience leaves with
  • Understand how overlooking the nervous system has shaped modern medicine
  • Appreciate the complex relationship between our nervous systems components
  • Instantly practical tools for improving neurological health
Talk 04
Built to Last
Sustainable energy, sleep and resilience for people who can't afford to slow down
Keynote Workshop 30–90 min

High performance is not about being endlessly available. It is about being genuinely effective. This session brings together latest evidence on sleep quality, energy management, recovery rhythms, and long-term resilience. David then translates it into a personal framework audience members can begin using immediately.

Particularly effective as a closing session or as part of a leadership development programme.

Audience leaves with
  • A personal energy audit and a clear picture of where capacity is challenged
  • Evidence-based optimisation tools that work in real professional lives
  • A sustainable performance framework to carry forward
Special Session
Who Looks After You?
A session built specifically for HR professionals — the people who carry everyone else
HR Teams 30–60 min Complimentary intro available

HR professionals are the most overlooked employees in most organisations. They spend their days building wellbeing systems for others while operating without equivalent support themselves. The emotional labour is substantial, largely invisible, and rarely acknowledged by leadership.

This session is designed specifically for HR teams. Built from original research conducted with senior HR professionals, and delivered with the clinical understanding of what chronic occupational stress actually does to body and mind.

A complimentary introductory session is available for People Teams of up to 20 people. This reflects a genuine belief that the people who are responsible for organisational wellbeing deserve to experience what good support actually feels like!

This session offers
  • Acknowledgement of the specific weight that HR professionals carry — without platitude
  • Clinical insight into what sustained occupational stress does physiologically
  • Practical tools tailored to the HR professional's specific context
  • A space to be honest that most workplaces don't provide
